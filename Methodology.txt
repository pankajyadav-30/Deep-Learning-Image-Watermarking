##Stage 1: Image Partitioning

GrabCut segmentation
Morphological dilation
Generation of:
* Object Mask
* Background Mask

##Stage 2: Watermark Embedding

Two independent autoencoders:

1. Object Autoencoder
2. Background Autoencoder

The watermark is embedded separately in both regions.

##Stage 3: Image Recombination

Merge object and background outputs
Generate final watermarked image
Preserve visual quality
